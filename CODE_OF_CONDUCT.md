# Community conduct

Reinvent It welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of computer-science experience. Apply these expectations in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained for the project.

## How we work together

Be curious, specific, and respectful. Critique a design or claim without attacking its author. Explain unfamiliar ideas patiently. Credit assistance and other people's work. Accept that independently discovering an established idea can be a successful result.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, sexualized content or unwanted attention, threats, impersonation, spam, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable. Do not use the project to promote unrelated material.

Protect the game as well: keep answers and leading hints out of public issue titles and ordinary feedback; respect weekly independence and the agreed reveal. Do not mock incomplete solutions or make unsupported claims of novelty.

## Moderation

The current maintainer, Ryan M. Frank, may ask for an edit, hide or remove inappropriate content, close or lock a disruptive conversation, or restrict participation. The response should match the severity and repetition of the behavior. Threats, exposed private information, or sustained harassment can justify immediate action without a warning.

For accidental spoilers, remove the revealing detail and continue with a spoiler-free summary. For accidentally posted private information, stop quoting or forwarding it, remove it where possible, and use the reporting routes below. Deleting a post cannot guarantee that copies disappear; exposed credentials also need revocation by their owner.

Explain moderation decisions when doing so will not further expose private information or amplify harm. Someone who disagrees may request reconsideration with new context; do not repeat removed content or mobilize harassment.

## Reporting

For an ordinary moderation question, open a short, non-sensitive issue or ask in the affected conversation. Include only the minimum public context needed.

Use GitHub's built-in reporting tools for harassment, threats, or privacy violations that should not be reposted publicly. Choose GitHub Support for platform abuse; if GitHub offers a separate report-to-repository-admins option, that route goes to the repository maintainers. See [reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

For a project security vulnerability or accidental exposure of credentials, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Security advisories are for security reports, not a general complaint inbox. The project currently has one maintainer and no independent moderation panel or guaranteed response time.
