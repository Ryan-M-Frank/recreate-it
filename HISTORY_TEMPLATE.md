## Selection rule

The GM compares both IDs and underlying fingerprints before selecting a solo challenge. A different story does not make an already encountered mechanism new. Unknown prior exposure must be recorded as unknown.

Do not prefill unrevealed targets in this file. A paused or weekly frozen record includes ID/version and progress, with post-reveal fields left empty. After an incomplete round's reveal, record exposure so it is not accidentally presented as fresh later.
