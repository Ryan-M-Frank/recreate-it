## Problem and solution

## Correctness

State invariants, reasoning, test traces, counterexamples, and remaining gaps. Distinguish tests from a proof.

## Costs and tradeoffs

Define input sizes and computational model. Cover initialization, time per operation, memory, maintenance, and worst-case versus amortized or expected claims.

## Reasoning path

Summarize independent insights, failed approaches, revisions, and the contribution of hints or other assistance. Include only game-relevant details.

## Known weaknesses

Record incomplete behavior, unsupported claims, and workloads that perform poorly.

## Post-reveal comparison — omit before reveal

Record known relatives and any formal analysis or prior-art review. An unresolved novelty flag is a request for investigation, not a claim of a new algorithm.
